# ReelForge AI: Copywriting Guidelines

The interface should sound calm, confident, and highly intelligent. We avoid overly enthusiastic "startup" jargon and robotic, overly technical system-speak. We speak like a senior Chief Marketing Officer or an expert producer.

## 1. Tone of Voice
- **Calm & Measured:** No exclamation marks unless congratulating a major milestone (e.g., successful onboarding).
- **Precise & Concise:** Use as few words as possible without losing meaning.
- **Confident:** The AI knows what it's doing. Don't use passive language.

## 2. Writing Examples by Category

### Headlines & Empty States
- **Avoid:** "Wow! It looks a little empty here! Let's get started on making some awesome content! 🚀"
- **Use:** "No blueprints generated yet. Start a new workflow to analyze your profile."
- **Reasoning:** Professional, direct, and provides a clear next step without fake enthusiasm.

### Button Labels
- **Avoid:** "Click Here to Generate", "Submit"
- **Use:** "Generate Blueprint", "Save Profile"
- **Reasoning:** Buttons should describe the exact action that will occur. Use `<Verb> <Noun>`.

### Error Messages
- **Avoid:** "Oops! Something went wrong on our end. Please try again later."
- **Use:** "Failed to generate script. The AI provider timed out. Click retry to resume the workflow."
- **Reasoning:** State the exact problem without infantilizing "Oops!" language, and provide a clear recovery path.

### Success Messages
- **Avoid:** "Success! Your post is saved perfectly!"
- **Use:** "Blueprint saved."
- **Reasoning:** The UI state (a green checkmark or toast) communicates the emotion. The text should just state the fact.

### AI System Messages (Thinking/Status)
- **Avoid:** "Thinking really hard...", "Gathering the magic..."
- **Use:** "Analyzing competitor engagement...", "Retrieving L5 Audience Context...", "Generating script..."
- **Reasoning:** Exposing the actual steps the AI is taking builds trust and reinforces the idea of a complex, structured "Intelligence Engine" rather than a black-box magic trick.

## 3. Formatting Rules
- **Sentence Case:** Use sentence case for all headings, labels, and buttons (e.g., "Generate blueprint", not "Generate Blueprint"), unless it refers to a proper noun or specific ReelForge branded term (e.g., "Blueprint Studio"). *(Note: Actually, standard SaaS practice leans towards Title Case for buttons, but Apple and modern interfaces often prefer Sentence Case. For ReelForge, we will standardize on **Title Case** for primary Buttons and Tabs, and **Sentence Case** for all body text and descriptions.)*
- **Punctuation:** No periods at the end of button labels or short bullet points.
